title: How to fix a noisy voice recording before you start editing slug: fix-noisy-voice-recordings date: 2026-10-14 category: Audio cleanup read: 6 description: A simple order of steps for cleaning dialogue with AI noise reduction, and the mistakes that make a voice sound fake. ---

Short version. Clean the audio once, on the original file, before you cut. Use AI noise reduction at a medium setting, check the result on two kinds of speakers, then put a little room tone back under the dialogue so the voice does not sound cut out of the room.

Start with the right file

AI cleanup tools work best on the original recording. A video that has already been compressed and uploaded somewhere has lost detail the tool needs, so ask for the source audio whenever you can.

Clean the full clip before you cut it up. That keeps the noise level the same across every cut, and you avoid one shot sounding different from the next.

Know what kind of noise you have

Different problems need different fixes. Listen to the clip on headphones and match it to the table before you pick a tool.

What you hearWhat usually helps
Constant hiss, a fan, or an air conditionerAI noise reduction works well here. Start at a medium setting.
Low rumble or windApply a high-pass filter first, then noise reduction.
Echo from an empty roomDereverb tools help a little. Results vary a lot, so judge by ear.
Traffic, a crowd, or music behind the voiceVoice isolation tools. Listen for a watery, underwater sound on S and T.
Distortion from a recording that was too loudVery hard to fix. Re-record if you can.

Tools that clean dialogue

Most editors now include a speech cleanup feature, so you may not need a separate tool.

  • Adobe Premiere Pro has Enhance Speech, and Adobe Podcast offers a web version of the same idea.
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio has Voice Isolation. It is part of the paid Studio version, not the free one.
  • Descript has Studio Sound, which fits well if you also edit by transcript.

Feature names, limits, and prices change often. Check the tool's current page before you subscribe.

The order that works

  1. Duplicate the original audio track so you can switch back and compare.
  2. Add a high-pass filter somewhere around 70 to 80 Hz to remove rumble. Voices rarely need anything below that.
  3. Run the AI noise reduction or voice isolation. Start at a medium strength, not the maximum.
  4. Listen on headphones, then on a phone speaker. A fix that sounds fine in one place can sound strange in the other.
  5. Lay a few seconds of the room's own quiet under the dialogue at a very low level. This is called room tone, and it stops the gaps between sentences from sounding dead.
  6. Set the loudness last. Most online platforms normalize around -14 LUFS, and dialogue-led videos commonly sit between -14 and -16.

Mistakes that make a voice sound fake

  • Running the effect at full strength. Breaths disappear and S sounds turn watery.
  • Stacking two tools. Cleaning twice usually adds more damage than it removes noise.
  • Leaving complete silence between lines. Real rooms are never silent. Room tone fixes this.
  • Checking on one pair of headphones only. Always test on a second playback device.

When to re-record instead

If the voice is distorted, or the noise is another person talking, no tool will fully fix it. A few pickup lines recorded later are usually cheaper than hours of repair work, and they sound better.
