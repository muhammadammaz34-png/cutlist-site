{{name}} is a small site about AI tools for video editors and content creators. It covers what the tools do well, where they fall short, and how to fit them into a normal edit.

I'm {{author}}. I'm a video editor, and I've worked on corporate videos, documentaries, drama, and social media content. AI now shows up in almost every part of that work: cleaning audio, writing captions, cutting out backgrounds, and finding the best takes. Some of it saves hours. Some of it wastes your time. I write about both.

What you will find here

  • Step by step guides for specific jobs, like fixing noisy audio or making accurate captions.
  • The settings worth changing and the mistakes that make a result look fake.
  • Plain comparisons, including cases where the free option is the better choice.

What you will not find

Lists of fifty tools with no opinion. Every guide is about getting one job done well.

Corrections

Tools change quickly, so every article shows the date it was last checked. If something is out of date or wrong, please tell me and I will fix it.
