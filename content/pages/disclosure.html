Advertising

The site may show ads from Google AdSense. Ads are placed by Google, and I do not choose which advertisers appear. Ads are separate from the articles, and advertisers have no say in what is written.

Affiliate links

Some links to software may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked in the article where they appear.

Whether a tool has an affiliate program is never a reason to include it. If a free tool is the better choice, the guide says so.

Free access and review accounts

If a company gives free access to a paid tool for review, the article says so. It does not get a different standard than any other tool.
