This policy explains what information {{name}} (the "site") collects when you visit it and how that information is used.

Information the site collects

The site has no accounts, comments, or forms, and it does not ask you for personal details. Like most websites, the hosting provider keeps server logs. These can include your IP address, browser type, the pages you visited, and the time of your visit. The logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

The site may use an analytics service to count visits and see which pages are read. This data is used in aggregate to improve the site.

Cookies and advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this site and to other sites on the internet.

You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, but the ads you see may be less relevant.

Links to other sites

Articles link to tools and websites run by other people. Some of these links may be affiliate links, as explained on the disclosure page. This policy does not cover those sites, and each has its own privacy practices.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data is held about you, or to ask for it to be deleted. The site holds very little personal data, but if you want to make a request, contact me.
